Questions

Good to know.

Is LifeTrak a free expense tracker?

Yes — completely free. Unlimited events and expenses, family sharing, and CSV export, at no cost.

Can I track wedding expenses with my family?

Yes. Create a wedding event, set a budget, and share it with an invite link. Everyone logs what they paid, advances get adjusted with negative entries, and you all see one honest total.

Does LifeTrak work offline?

Yes. Expenses added with no signal are saved on your phone and sync automatically when you're back online — built for travel and patchy networks.

Can I log expenses in foreign currencies?

Yes. Log in baht, dollars, euros and more — LifeTrak converts at live rates into your home currency, so your trip has one total.

Do I need an app store to install it?

No. LifeTrak installs straight from the browser to your home screen on Android and iPhone, and runs full-screen like a native app.
